The round coral key that starts and stops a take; the only place `record` appears.

- 88px circle with a 5px `record-edge`; sinks when pressed.
- While recording: stop square, and a live waveform of teal bars beside the timer (it replaces a plain level meter and is this product's signature motion).
- Under the waveform, words are required: *Too quiet / Good level / Too loud*.
- Timer `m:ss / 3:00` in Fredoka 26/600, tabular.
- After stop: *Listen back* (secondary) and *Save take* (primary). Space toggles.
