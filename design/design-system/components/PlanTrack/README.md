The 14-session plan drawn as a sound wave: each session is a bar, the bars rise toward the check-in.

- Done: `sun` bars. Today: `primary` with a ring. Ahead: `surface`. Check-in: a dashed `sky` bar labelled *Test*.
- Bar heights are decorative rhythm (they rise overall), not a score — they never encode measured improvement.
- Header names the focus from the two priorities (*Pace and pauses*) and the session count and check-in date in words.
- One primary key under it: *Start session 6*. After a check-in, a new wave (plan version) starts; the old one moves to Progress.
