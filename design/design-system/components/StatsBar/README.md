Pill chips at the top of Home and Session: the streak wave, notes collected, minutes practiced; plus today's bar.

- **Streak** — a rising sound-wave glyph in `sun-edge` with *N days*: consecutive days with a finished session (user's timezone). At 0 it just says *Start a streak today*.
- **Notes** — the reward unit (a teal music note): 2 per warm-up, 3 per target exercise, +1 for a take that passes the quality check. They reward practice, never the voice.
- **Minutes** — total practice time.
- Today's bar counts exercises, not points.
- All of it lives in the guest session: *Your streak lives in this browser.*
