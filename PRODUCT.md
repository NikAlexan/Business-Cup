# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

US market, ages 25–45, English UI. Voice-over artists, voice actors, content creators, and people who want a more expressive, narrator-like voice. Their job: daily voice and diction practice toward narrator-quality delivery, on their own, without a coach.

## Product Purpose

Resonance records the user's voice, measures what it can reliably measure, and builds a personal 14-day plan from a fixed exercise catalog. Daily sessions take 5–8 minutes. Every 14 days a check-in repeats the recording protocol, shows observed changes, and updates the plan. Success: the user understands where their delivery is relative to the narrator target, practices daily, and sees an honest comparison at check-in.

## Positioning

Real recording → measured metrics plus an AI review tied to those measurements → a 14-day plan from a fixed, validated catalog → a check-in that honestly reports whether anything changed, including "no confident change". Not speech therapy, no promises of improvement.

## Operating Context

Guest session in one browser (cookie, no account). Flow: choose a goal (clear speech, expressive voice, confident delivery) → mic check → baseline recording (three tasks, up to 3 minutes) → voice profile and plan → daily exercise → progress and streak → check-in after 14 days. Chrome on desktop, Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Measured: pace (wpm with and without pauses), pauses (count, duration, share), pitch (median, p10–p90 range), intonation range (p10–p90 in semitones). Timbre and diction are qualitative AI observations only.
- An unreliable metric is shown as unavailable, never as zero. AI never changes computed numbers.
- Pitch is reference information, not a score: lower or higher pitch is never better. Growth or decline of a number is not declared improvement by itself.
- No diagnoses, no correction of speech disorders (lisp etc. out of scope).
- Exercises are referenced by name in the UI, never by internal id (E01…E12).
- Payments are not implemented; DEMO_MODE must be visibly labeled.

## Brand Commitments

Product name: Resonance.

## Evidence on Hand

Reference ranges for the voice profile, from sources (see conversation 2026-11-14, voice profile redesign):
- Pace: listeners prefer ~150 wpm; comprehension stable 130–190 (speech-rate study, ScienceDirect 2025). Audiobook ~150–160 wpm is a secondary-source figure.
- Pauses: ~14 % of casual reading time, mean pause ~0.5 s (Matzinger, Ritt & Fitch, PLOS ONE 2020); inter-breath-group pause ~0.65 s (Wang et al. 2010); about one pause per 7–8 words at normal rate (Werner et al., Speech Prosody 2022).
- Intonation: the p10–p90 f0 range best tracks perceived charismatic delivery (Niebuhr & Skarnitzl, ICPhS 2019). No numeric target exists; compare with the user's own recordings.
No testimonials, customers, or improvement statistics exist; do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Narrator delivery is the target; show the distance to it, not a rank among average people.
2. Every observation points to a measurement or a specific moment in the recording.
3. Honest over flattering: unavailable stays unavailable, no change is a valid result.
4. Coach, not clinician: describe, never diagnose.
